# Sage & Terracotta — Minimalist Boho Printable Wall Art Set of 6

A ready-to-list Etsy digital product, designed from research on Etsy's
current top-selling wall-art printables.

## Why this product

Research (Sept 2026) shows the strongest-selling printable niches on Etsy are:

1. **Abstract art prints** — ~90,000+ monthly searches
2. **Botanical & nature prints** — ~40,000+ monthly searches
3. **Boho / neutral / mid-century line art** — dominates Etsy's live "boho" market pages
4. The 2026 trend signal is **"quiet luxury" muted botanicals** in cream, sage, and terracotta

Top sellers win with **coordinating gallery-wall sets** (3–12 matching prints
drive the highest average order value). This product hits that exact formula:
a cohesive 6-design set in a single locked palette and style.

All art is **originally generated** for this project (inspired by the *style*
of top sellers — not copied from any seller's work).

## How to list it on Etsy (quick start)

1. Copy the listing copy from [`Etsy-Listing.md`](Etsy-Listing.md) — title, 13 tags, description, pricing.
2. Upload the listing images from `listing/` in this order:
   `cover.jpg` → `design-1..6.jpg` → `mockup-1.jpg` → `size-guide.jpg` → `whats-included.jpg`.
3. Attach the **two ZIPs** from `package/` as the digital download files.
4. Set it as a **Digital Download**, choose `Home & Living > Home Decor > Wall Decor > Prints > Digital Prints`, and publish.

## Rebuilding / customizing

- `masters/` — swap any design by replacing the matching file (keep 4:5 portrait, ~928×1152+).
- `listing-source/` — drop more AI lifestyle mockups here; they're auto-sized into `listing/`.
- Run `bash build.sh && bash package.sh` to regenerate everything.

## Honest quality notes (for you, the seller)

- Native art is ~928×1152 px (1 MP), then upscaled with Lanczos to 300 DPI.
  The flat vector + paper-grain style upscales cleanly. Sharpest prints are
  **up to ~11×14 in / A4**; larger sizes (16×20, 24×36) will print fine but
  slightly softer. This matches the quality of most AI-generated Etsy listings.
- The two ZIPs stay under Etsy's 20 MB-per-file upload limit.
