HOW TO PRINT
------------
1. Choose the folder that matches the frame you own.
2. Print at 100% scale (do not "fit to page") on matte photo paper,
   cardstock, or cotton fine-art paper — 180 gsm or heavier looks best.
3. For sizes bigger than your home printer, upload the matching file to
   a local print shop or an online lab.
