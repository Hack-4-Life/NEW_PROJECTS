## 3. Description

> Paste the block below exactly. Etsy strips most formatting, so the structure
> uses uppercase headings, dashes and arrows rather than markdown.

```
A budget dashboard that just tells you the truth — how much you can actually spend
today, without touching a single bill.

No account to create. No monthly fee. No formulas to build. One file, opened in
any browser, working whether you are online or off.

★ THE ONE NUMBER THAT RUNS EVERYTHING
Open it and the first thing you see is your safe-to-spend figure: your real cash
minus every bill, debt minimum and savings deposit due before your next payday,
minus a buffer you choose. Tap "Show the math" and it shows you every line of the
calculation — no black boxes, no spreadsheets to trust blindly.

★ WHAT'S INSIDE (8 working screens)
• TODAY — safe-to-spend, a 45-day cash forecast, smart alerts, this month at a glance
• SPEND — envelope budgets, daily pacing, full searchable transaction log, top merchants
• BILLS — every recurring bill on one list and one calendar, one-tap "paid", skip or pause a cycle
• DEBTS — snowball vs avalanche payoff planner with the exact debt-free date and the interest you avoid
• GOALS — savings goals with progress rings, finish dates and a funding check that tells you if the plan is realistic
• CALENDAR — bills, income and spending on one month at a glance
• REPORTS — income vs spending trends, net worth, category averages, a 100-point money score
• SETTINGS — any currency, weekly/fortnightly/twice-monthly/monthly pay, buffer size, five themes

★ THINGS OTHER BUDGET TRACKERS DON'T DO
• Safe-to-spend, not just "spent so far"
• A 45-day cash forecast so a tight week never surprises you
• It finds your subscriptions on its own — same merchant, same amount, steady interval
• Envelope pacing that understands bills, so rent never looks like overspending
• A funding check that compares your savings goals to your actual surplus
• Privacy goggles that blur every amount when you are on a train
• Undo on everything

★ NO INSTALLS, NO SUBSCRIPTION, NO CATCH
• Works entirely offline — aeroplane mode, USB stick, café wifi that never works
• Your data stays in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, ever
• No account, no email, no app store, no ads, no premium tier
• Mac, Windows, Linux, iPad, Android tablet — anything with a browser
• Buy once, use it for years, and every future update is free

★ WHAT YOU GET INSTANTLY
• Waypoint-Budget-Studio.html — the complete dashboard in one file
• Getting-Started-Guide.html — a full illustrated manual (also prints beautifully)
• READ-ME-FIRST.txt — three steps to be running, and how to back up
• LICENSE.txt — plain-English personal use terms
• Plus a 3-step guided welcome, a built-in tour, and a full sample budget you can
  explore before entering your own numbers

★ HOW IT ARRIVES
This is an instant digital download. Your files are ready the moment payment
clears — no shipping, nothing to wait for. Open the folder, double-click the HTML
file, and you are budgeting.

★ A NOTE ON YOUR DATA (worth 30 seconds)
Everything lives in your own browser, which is exactly what makes it private — and
means it stays with that browser on that computer. The dashboard includes a
one-click backup file so you can move everything to a new computer or browser in
ten seconds, and it will remind you every month. Your data is yours to keep,
inspect and move.

★ QUESTIONS
Message me any time — I answer quickly, and if anything isn't working I will make
it right. This is a personal budgeting tool, not financial advice.

Thanks for supporting a small shop. Enjoy seeing the whole picture. ♥
```
